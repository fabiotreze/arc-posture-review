## Feather Icons

Some SVG icons in the browser app, in the saved analyses and in the v4 report (side menu, section and summary icons) are copied or adapted from [Feather](https://github.com/feathericons/feather) (<https://feathericons.com>).

- **Copied unchanged:** `alert-triangle`, `box`, `check`, `check-square`, `copy`, `flag`, `git-branch`, `git-pull-request`, `grid`, `layers`, `search`, `shield`, `trending-up`.
- **Adapted:** `file-text` (without its last line), `alert-circle` (smaller radius) and `search` with an added mark (the *not verified* icon).
- **Where:** `assurance/v4/checkpoint6/report/render_v4.py` and `tools/report-ux.js` (synchronized into the HTML pages). The comparison was made against the `main` branch of the Feather repository on 2026-11-06.
