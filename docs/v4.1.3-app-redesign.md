# v4.1.3 — app redesign

The goal: open the Analysis and know in ten seconds where you stand and what to do first, without false alarms.

## What changed

- **Verdict** at the top of the Analysis: *Verified gaps* (FAIL), *Improvement opportunities* (WARN) and *Not verified* (TODO, PARTIAL, ERROR) as three numbers, a stacked bar that also shows what meets the criterion and what is informational, and **Do first**: the three actions with the biggest gain, each linking to its detail. An improvement opportunity is a hardening or visibility step that is not configured; it does not mean a practice is insecure, and *Not verified* is a collection task, not a finding.
- **Start here** at the top of the Guide: the three steps (Collect, Open, Deliver) as buttons that jump to each step, and a search box that filters the guide sections (for example `CHK-23`, `extension`, `baseline`).
- **Default scope**: the wizard now starts on *Subscription list (recommended)*. *Current subscription only* stays available and warns that it queries just the subscription `az login` uses by default.
- **Theme**: cleaner light palette, softer cards and buttons, clearer focus states; dark mode and print keep working.

## Not changed

Collector logic, the 64 checks, the 11 scenarios and the `assessment-result.v4` contract are unchanged. The verdict only regroups statuses and gains the page already computes.

## Where it lives

`tools/report-ux.js` (verdict, Start here, search), `tools/report-responsive.css` (theme), embedded into the app pages by `tools/Sync-ReportAssets.py`.
